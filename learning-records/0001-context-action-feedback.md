# Context, action, and selected-action feedback

The learner correctly identified the router's context as pre-action task information, its action as the selected execution strategy, and its feedback as the quality of the chosen strategy's result. They also recognized that online replay cannot update unchosen strategies because those outcomes were not observed. The next lesson should sharpen the distinction between a one-step bandit episode and full RL's action-dependent state transitions; the learner was uncertain about that point.
