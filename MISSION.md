# Mission: Contextual Bandits for the Adaptive Agent Router

## Why
Understand the reinforcement-learning ideas in this project well enough to explain and defend the router, then use that understanding to run credible experiments and improve the implementation.

## Success looks like
- Explain why this is a contextual bandit rather than full reinforcement learning.
- Trace context, action, evaluation, reward, exploration, and policy updates through the code.
- Implement or modify epsilon-greedy, UCB, and LinUCB without treating unchosen actions as observed.
- Design and interpret experiments involving quality, cost, latency, regret, and distribution shift.

## Constraints
- Learn through this repository and short, interactive lessons.
- Prioritize practical understanding over unnecessary mathematical breadth.
- Do not fine-tune LLM weights or build large-scale RL infrastructure.

## Out of scope
- Full RL for multi-step control problems.
- Policy-gradient training of language models.
- Production-scale distributed bandit systems until the small experiment is sound.
